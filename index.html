01PROFILE

OPERATIONS SHOULD BE LEGIBLE

Reliable systems are built in the details between teams.

I’m a DevOps Engineer working across CI/CD, cloud infrastructure, containers, and observability. I care about the parts of shipping software that are easy to overlook: how a change is built, how it reaches production, and how the team can tell it is healthy once it is there.

Day to day, that means Jenkins pipelines, Docker services released with blue‑green deployment, secrets kept in HashiCorp Vault, and metrics, logs, and alerts in Prometheus, Grafana, and Loki. I aim for setups that are repeatable, observable, and easy to recover, and documented well enough for someone else to run.

05PRINCIPLES

HOW I APPROACH THE WORK

Build for the
day after launch.

Operational reliability comes from deliberate decisions made before, during, and after every release.

  1. 01

    Repeat the right things.

    Automate recurring tasks and document the procedures operators rely on.

  2. 02

    Plan the way back.

    Keep rollback procedures, backups, and recovery paths part of operational planning.

  3. 03

    Follow the evidence.

    Use metrics, logs, and monitoring signals to investigate behavior and troubleshoot production issues.

  4. 04

    Make access intentional.

    Apply deliberate identity and permission controls, and manage secrets with appropriate access boundaries.
