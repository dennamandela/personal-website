05PRINCIPLES

HOW I APPROACH THE WORK

Build for the
day after launch.

Operational reliability comes from deliberate decisions made before, during, and after every release.

  1. 01

    Repeat the right things.

    Automate recurring tasks and document the procedures operators rely on.

  2. 02

    Plan the way back.

    Keep rollback procedures, backups, and recovery paths part of operational planning.

  3. 03

    Follow the evidence.

    Use metrics, logs, and monitoring signals to investigate behavior and troubleshoot production issues.

  4. 04

    Make access intentional.

    Apply deliberate identity and permission controls, and manage secrets with appropriate access boundaries.
